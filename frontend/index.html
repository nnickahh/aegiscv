AI Emergency Coordinator

“See the situation. Understand the risk. Coordinate the response.”

An autonomous, human-governed emergency command system that watches live or uploaded video streams with OpenCV 5 (dense optical flow, posture aspect-ratio collapse, HSV/YCrCb thermal flame & aerosol segmentation, crowd convex-hull clustering, and polygon intrusion testing) and closes the loop with a stateful AI Coordinator Agent that dynamically adjusts vision parameters, quantifies uncertainty, escalates or reverses decisions as the scene changes, and orchestrates response via AWS.

01 / SEE
OpenCV 5 Perception
Multi-cue pixel analysis: people tracking, posture aspect ratio, Farneback optical flow, fire/smoke contours, crowd hulls & polygon zones.
02 / UNDERSTAND
Context & Active Vision
Agent evaluates temporal kinematics & confidence. When uncertain, commands OpenCV ROI Zoom + CLAHE to verify micro-motion.
03 / PRIORITIZE
Dynamic Risk Scoring
Computes live severity (0–100) across single or compound crises. Dynamically escalates or downgrades if the subject recovers.
04 / ACT
Response & HITL Governance
Creates DynamoDB incidents, archives S3 evidence frames, recommends actions, and requests Human-in-the-Loop approval for critical dispatches.
05 / VERIFY
Closed-Loop Verification
Re-scans the incident zone via OpenCV to confirm hazard containment, self-recovery, or responder arrival—closing the perception-action loop.

              01 · PERCEPTION LAYER (backend/opencv_pipeline.py)
            

            

              OpenCV 5 Real-Time Vision Engine
            

            
    
              
  • Dense Optical Flow: cv2.calcOpticalFlowFarneback computes pixel velocity & kinetic energy on every frame.
    • 
              
  • Multi-Cue Person & Fall Fusion: Combines MOG2 foreground masks, HOGDescriptor people detector, Haar Cascades, and aspect-ratio ($w/h$) inversion tracking.
    • 
              
  • Fire & Smoke Segmentation: Multi-threshold HSV + YCrCb flame core + rising desaturated plume analysis.
    • 
              
  • Spatial Polygon & Crowd Hull: cv2.pointPolygonTest for Sector-B 13.8kV breaches & cv2.convexHull for crowd surge density.
    • 
              
  • Active Re-Scan Kernels: cv2.createCLAHE (LAB contrast recovery) + 2.4x ROI Picture-in-Picture micro-motion verification.
    • 
            

          


          
          

            

              02 · AGENTIC LOOP (backend/ai_agent.py)
            

            

              See → Understand → Prioritize → Act → Verify
            

            
    
              
  • Bidirectional CV Control: When confidence is ambiguous ($0.52\text{–}0.75$), the Agent actively commands OpenCV 5 via REQUEST_ROI_ZOOM_ANALYSIS or BOOST_SENSITIVITY_CLAHE before escalating.
    • 
              
  • Dynamic Decision Reversal: If OpenCV detects a fallen subject standing back up with restored optical flow velocity, the Agent autonomously downgrades priority and cancels EMS dispatch.
    • 
              
  • Multi-Hazard Triage Scoring: Computes composite 0–100 priority scores and ranks life-safety extraction #1 during compound emergencies.
    • 
              
  • Human-in-the-Loop Gate: High-consequence dispatches pause for operator authorization (`Approve` / `ROI Re-Scan` / `Dismiss`).
